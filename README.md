# 🌍 TZ7 Life

**A living world. Your life. Your choices.**

TZ7 Life is an ambitious 3D open-world life simulation project by TZ7 Stack.

## 🎮 Vision

Build a persistent, evolving world where players can create their lives, explore countries, work, travel, manage money, interact with governments and experience a simulated global society.

## 🌐 Planned Features

- Realistic 3D open-world environments
- Third-person and overhead camera modes
- Jobs, careers, housing and everyday life
- Global travel and country exploration
- Multiple currencies and simulated exchange rates
- Banking, trade and economic simulation
- Governments, elections and diplomacy
- Law-enforcement agencies and investigations
- International organisations and cooperation
- Persistent world state and player saves
- Future multiplayer support

## 🛠️ Technology

- **Engine:** Godot 4
- **Language:** GDScript
- **Renderer:** Compatibility
- **Initial platform:** Windows PC
- **Future goal:** Browser support and multiplayer

## 🚀 Development Roadmap

- [ ] Version 0.1 — Playable 3D neighbourhood
- [ ] Version 0.2 — Player interactions, money and saving
- [ ] Version 0.3 — Countries, currencies and exchange rates
- [ ] Version 0.4 — Travel and regional simulation
- [ ] Version 0.5 — Economy, government and elections
- [ ] Version 0.6 — Agencies and international organisations
- [ ] Version 0.7 — Persistent online world prototype

## 📌 Project Principles

- Start small and expand gradually.
- Keep the game playable on modest hardware.
- Distinguish real-world reference data from simulated events.
- Respect the laws, cultures and institutions of different countries.
